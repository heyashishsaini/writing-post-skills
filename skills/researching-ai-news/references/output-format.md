# Output Format

Use this template for compiled AI news. Replace the date with today's real date.

```markdown
## AI News - [Date]

### Company Updates
- **[Company]**: [Brief summary of news] ([Source](url))

### Product Launches
- [Product/feature description] ([Source](url))

### Research & Breakthroughs
- [Research summary] ([Source](url))

### Regulation & Policy
- [Policy development] ([Source](url))

### Enterprise Adoption
- [Company/industry implementation story] ([Source](url))

### Industry Trends
- [Trend/funding/partnership] ([Source](url))
```

## Rules

- 1-2 sentences per story, in your own words
- A source link on every story; prefer the official source
- Empty category: write "No significant updates"
- `[DEVELOPING]` prefix for breaking or evolving stories
- `[UNCONFIRMED]` prefix for single-source or unverified stories
- Order inside each section by significance (major model releases first, then policy, funding, research, enterprise adoption, product updates)
- Consolidate duplicates; mention when many outlets cover the same story
